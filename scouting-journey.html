My Scouting Journey

From an eight-year-old Cub to regional youth leadership

What started as a childhood adventure slowly became one of the biggest foundations of who I am today.

2012

Cub Scout

I joined Scouting when I was eight years old, as a young Cub who probably did not yet understand how much this movement would shape his life. Through games, badges, promises and friendships, I discovered the joy of belonging to something bigger than myself.

Becoming a Sixer gave me my first taste of leadership. It was simple, but meaningful: helping others, taking responsibility, and learning that leadership begins with service. Attending my Cuboree made me realise that Scouting was much larger than my own pack.

2015

Scout Troop

Moving into the Scout Troop opened the door to real adventure. Camps became more challenging, hikes became longer, and every activity demanded more independence.

The 2016 Jamboree was one of the moments that changed how I saw Scouting. Being surrounded by so many Scouts helped me understand the scale and spirit of the Movement. Later, I became APL, PL and eventually SPL, learning that a good leader does not simply give instructions — he helps his patrol succeed together.

2019

Venture Scout

I joined the Venture Unit in 2019, excited for what many describe as one of the most memorable stages of the Scout journey.

But this chapter was cut short by COVID-19. Activities stopped, plans changed, and Scouting had to adapt. Even if my Venture years were shorter than expected, they taught me resilience and reminded me that Scouting is not only lived in camps and meetings, but also through the values we carry every day.

2023

Adult Leader

In 2023, my role changed completely. I was no longer only participating in activities — I became responsible for helping create meaningful experiences for younger Scouts.

Supporting the troop, mentoring young leaders, helping rebuild the Patrol Leaders’ Council and guiding Scouts through their own progression gave me a deeper appreciation of what adult leadership really means. Watching young people grow in confidence has become one of the most rewarding parts of my journey.

2024–2026

District Youth Scout Council President

Serving as President of the District Youth Scout Council of Port Louis widened my understanding of leadership beyond my own group. It became about creating spaces where young people could speak, lead, organise and be trusted.

Through district initiatives, youth activities, forums and projects, I learned that youth participation is not just about giving young people a seat at the table. It is about preparing them, trusting them, and building systems where their voices can genuinely influence decisions.

2025–2028

Africa Region Youth Representative

Today, my journey continues at regional level as Youth Representative for the Africa Scout Region. It still feels surreal that a path which began as an eight-year-old Cub now allows me to contribute to youth participation across Africa and World Scouting.

Through my thematic work around SDGs and Global Frameworks, I continue to learn, represent, and serve. This chapter is still being written, but it reminds me that every responsibility I hold today was built from the lessons, people and moments that shaped me along the way.
