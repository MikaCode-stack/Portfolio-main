Organisational Structure

Five levels, one Movement

Scouting in Mauritius runs on a clear chain of governance and support.

Level 01 · National

Mauritius Scouts Council

+
Description

The Council is the supreme governing body of the Association. It brings together all constituent members to set overall direction, approve policy and elect national leadership.

Sub-levels & key roles
Chief Scout Council Members Annual General Meeting
Level 02 · National

National Scout Board

+
Description

The Board provides strategic oversight between Council sessions, safeguarding the Association's mission, governance and long-term development.

Sub-levels & key roles
Board Chairperson Trustees Standing Committees
Level 03 · National

National Executive Committee

+
Description

The Executive Committee turns strategy into action — coordinating national programmes, commissioners and support functions across the Association.

Sub-levels & key roles
Chief Commissioner National Commissioners Support Committees
Level 04 · District

District Executive Committee

+
Description

Each District Executive Committee coordinates activities, training and events for the Groups within its district — the link between national structures and local Groups.

Sub-levels & key roles
District Commissioner District Committee Members District Events
Level 05 · Local

Local Scout Groups

+
Description

Groups are where Scouting happens day-to-day — Cub Packs, Scout Troops, Venture Units and Rover Crews delivering the programme directly to young people.

Sub-levels & key roles
Group Scout Leader Section Leaders Group Committee
